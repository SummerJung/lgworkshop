What it is

Not a competition. A reunion of play.

People from different countries form small pods and teach each other the games they grew up playing. No medals — just laughter and new friends.

🌍 Mixed pods

Different cultures in every group of 5–8.

🤸 Real, physical play

Run, chase, tag, hide. No screens.

🤝 Welcome over winning

We remember the moments, not the score.

Games from everywhere
